# Production of Disease-Free and Pest-Free Planting Materials

<img

One of the greatest risks in agriculture is the spread of plant diseases and pests through infected planting materials.
Once introduced into the field, pathogens can significantly reduce yields, increase chemical dependency, and cause 
long-term economic losses.

At **WEKS**, plant health begins in a sterile laboratory environment.

Our tissue culture processes are conducted under strictly controlled, contamination-free
conditions using advanced sterilization protocols,filtered air systems, and monitored 
environmental parameters. This ensures that plantlets are free from:

- Fungal infections
- Bacterial contamination
- Viral pathogens
- Soil-borne diseases
- Pest infestations

By starting with pathogen-free mother material and propagating within a sterile system, 
we eliminate the transmission of hidden infections that commonly affect traditional propagation.

The benefits for growers include:

- Stronger early-stage plant establishment
- Reduced mortality rates in the field
- Lower reliance on pesticides and fungicides
- Improved crop uniformity and productivity
- Higher long-term profitability
