# Year-Round, Climate-Independent Production

<img
  src="/assets/images/year-round.svg"
  alt="Large-Scale Mass Production of Elite Plant Varieties"
  className="mb-10"
/>

Agriculture is increasingly vulnerable to climate change, unpredictable weather patterns
, and seasonal variability. Traditional propagation systems are heavily dependent on rainfall, 
temperature cycles, and daylight availability.

At **WEKS**, we remove these limitations through controlled-environment production systems.

Our multi-layer indoor cultivation setup allows us to regulate:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Light intensity and photoperiod
- Air quality
- Sterility conditions

This fully controlled environment ensures consistent plant development regardless of external climate conditions.

The advantages of year-round production include:

- Continuous supply of planting materials
- Reduced seasonal production gaps
- Faster response to market demand
- Greater production planning stability
- Reduced vulnerability to climate shocks

By decoupling plant propagation from seasonal cycles, we create a resilient system capable of supporting
modern agricultural demands in both stable and challenging environments.

This climate-independent model strengthens food security and supports long-term agricultural sustainability.
